Absolute Scientific Integrity & Provenance Guarantee

TerraTwin adheres strictly to geospatial ground truth. All Mars and Earth elevation products are cryptographically verified via SHA-256 digests against official NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) and USGS / Copernicus archives. Coordinate reference systems (Equirectangular IAU2000:49900 and WGS84 EPSG:4326 / UTM Zone 43N) are explicitly preserved. No synthetic, hallucinated, or placeholder candidate coordinates are ever generated.
